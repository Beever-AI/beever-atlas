### ❗ Three things needing your decision

**1. "Supported by Animoca Brands."** Hosting at Animoca's office is confirmed; a support or
sponsorship relationship is not. Those are very different claims, and asserting the stronger one about
a company of that profile without a written arrangement is a real risk. If there is a formal
arrangement, say so and I'll restore the wording. Otherwise the copy below says "hosted at Animoca
Brands' Hong Kong office," which is accurate and still carries the association.

**2. Naming minors.** The copy names three children with their ages: Elyse (7), Ryder (12), Alia (15).
Alia and Ryder already have public footprints your own organisation created — press coverage and
LinkedIn credits — so naming them breaks nothing new. **Elyse, at 7, has no public record I could
find.** Publishing a 7-year-old's first name, exact age and role, tied to a named venue and a dated
event, is a bigger step than it looks. The publication title makes some naming unavoidable; consider
dropping her age, or confirming her guardians have agreed to this specific use.

**3. The registration mechanic.** "Comment Join, your IG ID will be used for registration" collects
children's social handles in a public comment thread visible to anyone. A DM-based or form-based
signup does the same job without the public list. Worth raising with whoever runs the account.
