## ⚠️ Two things in the slides to settle before posting

**1. The 97% / 67% accuracy figures are a device claim, not a Votee claim.**
The "Supported by AI-ready devices" slide attributes transcription accuracy to *voice input quality* —
roughly 67% from a consumer device outside the office, roughly 97% from a professional device inside
it — and the slide carries Jabra branding. Presenting those as Votee AI's transcription accuracy
would be a misattribution. The copy below frames it as input-device quality, which is what the slide
actually says. Confirm you're happy quoting a partner's device numbers at all before this ships.

**2. AI Insights sentiment analytics is not shipped yet.**
The "AI Insights — Reviews operation details at ease" slide carries the footnote **"To be available
in 2H 2026."** It is roadmap, not product. It is deliberately kept out of the posts below — announcing
it as available would be a false capability claim. Say the word if you want it included as an
explicitly-flagged "coming in 2H 2026" line instead.

## Notes

**What the photos added.** The first draft was written from the LinkedIn copy alone and was fairly
generic. The slides supply three concrete hooks that were missing: the dual cloud/on-prem modes under
one interface (which is squarely on Votee's sovereignty theme and is the strongest addition), the
named meeting-summary formats, and the point that input hardware bounds transcription quality.

**What changed from the LinkedIn original**

- **Instagram** drops all inline URLs — captions carry no clickable links — so the product block keeps
  the descriptions and routes to "Link in bio". The MAGIC YouTube link is cut for the same reason.
- **X** keeps every link, and stays **one post, never a thread**, per house style — so it needs a
  Premium account at this length.
- Eric Mok's title reads "Vice President at HKT" rather than the original's "Vice President @ HKT", so
  the `@` doesn't render as a broken handle mention.

**Worth your call**

- **Unicode-bold product names** are carried over to match house style. They render everywhere, but
  screen readers announce them character by character or skip them entirely — a real accessibility
  cost, and one that sits awkwardly beside writing proper alt text.
- **Tagging.** If you want Eric Mok, Jeff Tai or HKT actually tagged, their handles need confirming.
- **Attendee faces.** The room photo shows identifiable attendees. Check that seminar registration
  covered photography and social use before it goes out.
